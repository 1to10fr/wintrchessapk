## What's new in 1.1.0

### New
- App themes: Dark, Light and AMOLED black, plus a custom accent colour
- Six piece sets: Classic, Merida, Mono, Chessnut, Fantasy and Pixel
- Seven new board colour themes
- Board options: show or hide coordinates, and choose the last-move highlight and engine arrow colours
- Vibration on moves, captures and checks, with a switch in Settings
- Share a game as a PGN file or a position as an image

### Improved
- Taps and drags on the board are far more reliable
- Piece movement and engine analysis feel smoother
- Importing a PGN file works with .txt files on Android

### Fixed
- Settings no longer sends you back to the analysis page
- Black pieces and the evaluation bar are no longer recoloured by Android's dark mode
- Scrolling the analysis panel no longer selects moves by accident
